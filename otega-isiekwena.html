Managing Director

Otega Isiekwena

Brand strategy · Creative communications 7+ years of experience Lagos & Abuja, Nigeria info@cerebralhub.com

Otega Isiekwena is a brand strategist, creative communications professional and Managing Director of Cerebral & Dexter Media Limited, a brand design and creative communications agency focused on building distinctive brands through strategy, creativity and purposeful storytelling.

With over seven years of experience, Otega has worked across brand strategy, communications, creative development and media, helping organisations translate complex ideas into compelling brand experiences and meaningful audience connections.

Her professional experience includes working with notable organisations such as the Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC) and the Ford Foundation, among others, giving her a broad perspective on developing communication strategies for organisations operating across diverse sectors and audiences.

At Cerebral & Dexter Media, Otega provides strategic and creative leadership, guiding the agency’s approach to brand development, client engagement, creative direction and communications.

Her work is driven by the belief that effective brands are built where insight meets creativity, and creativity meets purpose.

She is passionate about building brands that are culturally relevant, strategically sound and visually distinctive, while creating work that delivers genuine value for both organisations and their audiences.

Under her leadership, Cerebral & Dexter Media continues to explore the intersection of brand design, storytelling, media and technology to create bold ideas and meaningful brand experiences.
